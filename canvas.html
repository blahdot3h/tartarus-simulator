How a canvas works

  1. The canvas. 6×6 cells: blank cells take paints, ice cells (blocked) and void cells don't. One Coloring Paint starts on the canvas, marked "!". A Treasure Chest is never on the canvas at the start, but about 1 canvas in 4 or 5 has one among your paints, usually at Lv2.
  2. The objective. The active Coloring Paint asks for paints of a type (monster, gameplay or any) in its range: Horizontal (its row), Vertical (its column), Diagonal (both its diagonals) or Adjacent (the 4 cells next to it). Paints already there count; the Coloring Paint itself doesn't, and its own objective doesn't raise it.
  3. Meeting it. Once enough are in range, every matching paint in range rises 1 level (more with Layer), to Lv7 at most. The Treasure Chest is a gameplay paint, so gameplay and "any" objectives that reach it raise it.
  4. The next Coloring Paint. A new one is offered. Pick a brushstroke direction: Reimagine (change its target or range), Spread (turn an ice cell blank, repair a broken cell, or place it on an ice, void, broken or painted cell, where it replaces the paint), or Layer (an extra effect when it's met). Then pick 1 of 2 effects, and place it. On a void cell its objective works from there, but it isn't a paint and doesn't take a blank cell.
  5. When stuck. Forced Color Mixing meets the objective right away, for Colored Snow (5, then 10, then 15; 3 a canvas). The price: 1 random paint on the canvas breaks. The paint is gone, and nothing can go on that cell until a Spread effect repairs it. It can be the chest.
  6. Starting over. For Colored Snow you can start the canvas over: the same canvas and the same paints, with new Coloring Paints and brushstrokes to come. With a chest in hand, that's how you fish for a Lv7 chest when the luck goes bad.
  7. The end. The canvas is done when its cells are full. An objective fails when its range has no blank cell left and it’s still short; paints that don’t match can go in its range without failing it, but they don’t rise. After a fail no new Coloring Paints come, so nothing levels up, unless you force the failed objective. Then the painting becomes a map, and a Lv7 chest is where the money is. Without a chest, aim for as many rainbow (Lv7) paints as you can: their eggs can drop skills too.
